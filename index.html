Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Resolution engine

tResolv classifies every incoming email, finds the right answer, and sends the reply automatically when confidence is high. No human needed for routine tickets.

86.5% resolution rate out of the box

Staged financial actions

Refunds, cancellations, exchanges, and address changes are always staged for your one-tap approval. They never execute automatically. Ever.

Refunds and cancellations always require your approval

Confidence scoring

Every AI draft shows a confidence percentage. You set where the autopilot threshold sits. Below that line, drafts queue for your review before anything is sent.

Fully configurable threshold

Gmail native

tResolv connects via Google OAuth and processes emails inside Google's own infrastructure. Your customer emails are never copied to or stored on tResolv's servers.

Your emails never leave Google

Smart quarantine

Promotions, auto-replies, and email loop risks are filtered before the AI sees them. Your resolution rate stays accurate and your inbox stays clean.

Stops email loops before they start

Order state awareness

Before drafting any reply, tResolv checks the live order state in Shopify. It will not attempt to cancel an already cancelled order, or refund one that has already been refunded. It knows what has already happened.

No duplicate actions, no embarrassing errors

Address auto-parsing

When a customer writes their new address in plain language, tResolv parses it into a clean structured format and stages it as a one-tap Shopify address update. You review the parsed address before it is applied.

Always staged for your approval before Shopify is updated
